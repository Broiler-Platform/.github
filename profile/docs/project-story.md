## How Broiler Started

Broiler began with a simple question:

> **Can a modern browser be built entirely in managed .NET?**

The common answer was always:

> "Use Chromium."

Broiler's answer remained:

> "No. Entirely in .NET."

Early experiments reused ideas from projects such as **HTML Renderer** and **YantraJS**. Those experiments demonstrated that a production-quality browser could not simply be assembled from existing components.

The architecture was gradually redesigned into reusable modules, and automated verification through Test262 and Web Platform Tests became part of the development workflow.

Over time another realization emerged: a browser and an office suite require many of the same foundational technologies. That insight transformed Broiler from a browser experiment into a broader application platform.

Today the project is moving from combined development snapshots toward separately versioned Preview Releases and reusable NuGet packages.
